# Round 1 — perk cards saying "already redeemed" (Sun Nov 8)

**~0.8h active.** Wall-clock time from this session's timestamps, about 3:30 to 4:20 PM PST, not measured
with the `time-accounting` script. Most of it was diagnosis; the code change itself is one constant.

## What happened

At 2:10 PM four 15-SFLUV perk-card scans arrived within 23 seconds while another payout was in flight. The
faucet dispatcher grouped them into two batches of two (`0xa1452cd428…` at 2:10:24 and `0xb9dc303afb…` at
2:11:56). A batch is a DELEGATECALL into Safe's `MultiSendCallOnly` (0x9641…02e2), and our paymaster
(0x825b77eE…5a55, owned by 0x2b5fa761…cfde) only sponsors whitelisted targets. Its whitelist was last set on
2026-09-21 and has never included `MultiSendCallOnly`, so both batches reverted on chain with "AA38 contract
not whitelisted". Batching was added on 2026-10-22, so no batch has ever succeeded; this was the first burst
close enough together to form one.

The submissions had reached the chain, so the payouts were held as "may have been sent" and the codes stayed
spent. The sweep marked them "not sent" at 3:11 PM (payout_ledger 745–748) but does not release codes, so
every rescan answered "already redeemed". The faucet held ~3,960 SFLUV throughout; the top-up at 2:14 PM was
a coincidence. The four people were paid by hand.

## The change

`maxFaucetBatch` 25 → 1 in `backend/handlers/protocol_faucet.go`, with the reason in the comment. Every payout
now takes the single-transfer path, which calls the token directly and is whitelisted. Bursts are slower:
about five seconds a payout, in order.
