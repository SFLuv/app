### A paid step that read as skipped — 0.20h · app

Reported: Jacky's payout did not show when exporting Finalized (Partial), though step 1 had been paid in
full.

Pulled from the admin MCP, then traced in the code:

- **Her workflow.** "Insta Post_Weekly Tenderloin Clean-up", week of Nov 2. Step 1 is `paid_out` to her,
  and 15 SFLUV reached her account (`0xeea0…44a1`). Step 2, the optional reel, is still `available`. The
  workflow is `skipped`.
- **Not only hers.** At least 24 workflows are in the same state, back to Jun 22, all in two weekly series:
  this one, and Tenderloin Weekly Clean-up Perk Card Coordination. None has a completed step still owed, so
  no money is stuck.
- **Why they were skipped.** When the next occurrence starts, the recurrence catch-up skipped the open one
  outright. Partial finalization was built for exactly this case, and would have skipped the unfinished
  steps and finalized the rest. It never saw them: the catch-up runs first in each maintenance pass, and
  partial finalization only looks at unfinished workflows.
- **Why the export hid it.** The export labelled every step of a skipped workflow "skipped", before looking
  at the step itself.
- **Also found, not fixed.** Recurrences are computed in UTC. Since clocks went back on Nov 1, this series
  starts at 23:00 on Sunday rather than midnight on Monday, so a date range starting on Monday misses that
  week's occurrence. Fixing it moves every recurring series by an hour, so it is left for its own change.

### Step 2 of the Perk Card Coordination, claimable again — 0.06h · app

Reported: step 2 is locked and cannot be claimed, since step 1 started being paid out and the series
started recurring.

- **The cause.** A locked step could be claimed only once the step before it was done. Here step 1, printing
  the cards, is finished on the Sunday, and the next occurrence starts that night. So step 2, a different
  role (Clean-up Coordinator), was claimable for a few hours a week. Nobody claimed it, so no claim reached
  the series and nobody held it from week to week. The current occurrence's step 2 is locked with nobody
  assigned.
- **The change.** A locked step can now be claimed once its workflow has started. It stays locked, and opens
  for its holder when the step before it is done. The claim endpoint and the improver board share this rule,
  so the board cannot show a Claim button the endpoint refuses. The improver page already offered Claim on
  locked steps; until now pressing it failed.

### Finished occurrences finalized, not skipped — 0.01h · app

- When the next occurrence starts, an open one with a completed or paid step now goes through partial
  finalization: its unfinished steps are skipped, and it becomes `paid_out` and `partially_completed`. One
  nobody finished a step in is still skipped, as before.
- The close-out is shared with the sweep (`finalizeWorkflowPartiallyTx`), which keeps creating its own
  successor; the catch-up creates its own as before.
- **A step completed but not yet paid** still blocks the close-out. The occurrence stays open, and the series
  waits for the payout before it moves on. Payouts usually land within seconds.
